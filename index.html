About Us

Welcome to Shree Bihariji Distributors Pvt Ltd. Based in Chaibasa, Jharkhand, we are a premier wholesale pharmaceutical distributor dedicated to supplying high-quality, authentic medicines to pharmacies, hospitals, and healthcare providers across the region.With a robust supply chain, strict temperature-controlled storage, and a comprehensive inventory of life-saving drugs and daily healthcare essentials, we ensure that critical medicines reach your business safely and on time. We are committed to fostering healthier communities by bridging the gap between leading pharmaceutical manufacturers and local healthcare systems with absolute reliability, transparency, and integrity.
